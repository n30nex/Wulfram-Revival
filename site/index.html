YOUR FIRST DEPLOYMENT

From download to repair pad.

No account registration. Pick a callsign and join the fight.

  1. 01

    Extract the client

    Download the Windows ZIP and extract the entire folder somewhere you can write files, such as your Games folder. Run Play Canadaverse.cmd from that folder.

    Keep the files together. Running from inside the ZIP will not work.

  2. 02

    Choose your callsign

    The launcher selects the Canadaverse server. Pick a unique callsign of 1–24 letters, numbers, spaces, dots, underscores or hyphens. Use canadaverse as the public player password.

    wulfram.canadaverse.org
  3. 03

    Join a team, choose a pad

    Click Crimson Federation or Azure Alliance, open Entry Map, then click a friendly repair-pad flag. If a pad is occupied, deployment uses another free friendly pad.

    The server keeps human spaces alongside its 64 bots.

CHOOSE YOUR VEHICLE

Tank. Scout. Siege.

Tanks bring versatile weapons. Support Scouts trade armour for a machine gun and a friendly repair beam. Siege uses the Tank chassis with extra armour and no cargo capacity.

Press Enter for chat, then use /s vehicle tank, /s vehicle scout or /s vehicle siege to choose your next deployment.

PLAY THE OBJECTIVE

A match worth finishing.

Vehicle kills, destroyed structures and Scout repairs earn team points. Timed rounds rotate battlefields; destroying the enemy's surviving resources can end a round earlier.

/s score shows the score. /s status shows the match. /s help lists available commands.

NEED A HAND?

Before you drop in.

The game won't connect.

Check the live status above, confirm the server address, and use the included launcher. The game needs outbound TCP and UDP port 2627. No port forwarding is needed on a player's computer.

The display or clicks look wrong.

Use Play Canadaverse.cmd. Opening wulfram2.exe directly bypasses the widescreen helper. Try the Balanced preset in Settings.cmd, then reopen the game. Report your display resolution and whether Windows scaling is enabled.

Is there a Linux client?

This first revival release targets Windows. A Linux client is not included.

Is this an official remaster?

No. This is a community revival using the preserved Wulfram II client, a reconstructed server and new Canadaverse additions. It is an early alpha; expect rough edges.
