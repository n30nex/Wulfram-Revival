CANADAVERSE WULFRAM II

1. Extract the entire ZIP into a writable folder, such as Games.
2. Open Play Canadaverse.cmd. Keep all the included files together.
3. Choose a unique callsign (1-24 letters, numbers, spaces, dots, _ or -).
   Public player password: canadaverse
4. Click a team, open Entry Map, then click a friendly repair-pad flag.

Server: wulfram.canadaverse.org
Player guide: https://canadaverse.org/wulfram/

MODERN CONTROLS
W/S: forward/reverse       A/D: strafe
Q/E: turn                 Space: jump jets
Mouse: aim                Left mouse: autocannon / Scout machine gun
Enter: chat               F3: radar range
F12: save a screenshot    Escape: game menu
1: pulse shell / Scout repair beam
2: Piercer (Tank/Siege)
Shift+Q / Z: increase/decrease upward thrust

Open Settings.cmd while the game is closed to choose classic controls or
Balanced/High graphics. Settings remain in the game folder. Previous settings
are retained under canadaverse/controls and canadaverse/graphics.

MATCH PLAY
64 CPU players join the two teams, with 16 additional places for people.
CPU roles include assault, flanking, guarding and interception.
Vehicle kills, destroyed structures and Scout repairs earn team points.
Rounds last up to 20 minutes and rotate between Crossroads, Mayhem and Frontier.

Press Enter and use these commands:
/s help                   available commands
/s score                  current score
/s status                 current match
/s vehicle tank           Tank for your next deployment
/s vehicle scout          Support Scout for your next deployment
/s vehicle siege          extra-armoured Tank; no cargo
/s vehicle auto           use the original map-screen vehicle selector

Support Scouts repair friendly units with their repair weapon. Friendly repair
and refuel pads restore your hull and fuel when you slow down close to the pad.
Frontier is an original map with three broad approaches and eight repair pads
per team. The other maps and artwork are from the preserved original game.

IF SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG
Always launch Play Canadaverse.cmd. Opening wulfram2.exe directly bypasses the
widescreen helper. Keep the D3D display system selected. Try Balanced graphics
if needed. The menus retain their original proportions; gameplay is widescreen.
The game needs outbound TCP and UDP port 2627. Players do not need router port
forwarding. Check the player page for server status and current downloads.

This is a community alpha, not an official remaster. Windows is the initial
supported client. Report your display resolution, Windows scaling and the exact
problem at https://github.com/n30nex/Wulfram-Revival/issues

Legacy squad services and persistent account ranks are not included. Your Stats
shows the current round; Overall shows this connection and resets on reconnect.
The original Units Info pages describe some equipment not yet implemented by
the revival server. Use the vehicle and weapon guide above for this release.
